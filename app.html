How to Install Your Signature
  1. Click "Copy Rich Signature" above.
  2. Open Outlook, and go to File > Options > Mail > Signatures.
  3. Click New, give it a name, and click inside the edit box.
  4. Paste using Ctrl+V (or right-click and paste keeping original formatting).
  5. Click OK to save.
