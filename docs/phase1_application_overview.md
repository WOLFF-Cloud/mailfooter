### 2. Business Purpose
MailFooter is a centralized, multi-tenant SaaS application designed to help businesses standardise, automate, and control their corporate email signatures. In addition, MailFooter transforms every outbound email sent by an organisation’s employees into a high-impact branding channel and active advertising space via dynamically updated marketing banners.

---

### 3. Business Problem Solved
1. **Brand Inconsistency**: In most organisations, employees manually set up their email signatures. This leads to mismatching fonts, outdated logos, broken links, non-compliant social icons, and overall damage to the corporate image. MailFooter enforces compliance by locking central brand assets.
2. **Untrackable Outbound Telemetry**: Daily email correspondence is one of the highest-volume touchpoints a business has with clients. Historically, organisations could not track the engagement generated by these emails. MailFooter tracks outbound email impressions (opens) and website click-through rates (CTR).
3. **High Campaign Overhead**: Swapping marketing banners manually (e.g., announcing a new event or product launch) requires every single employee to copy and reinstall their signature. MailFooter solves this by dynamically serving banner images from a central API; when the administrator swaps a banner, it is updated instantly across all active signatures without employee action.
4. **Email Layout Headaches**: Creating email signatures that render correctly across Outlook Desktop (known for its Microsoft Word-based rendering engine), Gmail, Apple Mail, and mobile devices is extremely difficult. MailFooter provides pre-tested HTML templates that look identical on both mobile and desktop screens.

### 4. Target Market & User Personas
MailFooter target markets are small-to-medium enterprises (SMEs), professional service firms, design/marketing agencies, and large corporate groups.

#### User Personas

| Persona Class | Target User | Role in System | Key Needs |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Staff Member (End User)** | Corporate Employee | Enters their direct details (Name, Job Title, Dept, Phone) and copies the generated footer. | Simplicity, zero technical friction, clear instructions for Outlook, Gmail, Apple Mail. |

### 5. Core Modules & Key Features

* **Public Landing Page (`index.html`, `landing.js`, `landing.css`)**: High-converting visual showcase featuring glassmorphic animations, pricing calculators, snap-slider team size estimators, and a modal login portal.
* **Staff Email Signature Builder (`app.html`, `script.js`, `styles.css`)**: Preloader-guarded generator with profile retrieval, interactive rendering preview (Desktop vs. Mobile toggle), and a 1-click clipboard compiler. Includes step-by-step installation walkthroughs for major email clients.
* **Tenant Admin Portal (`admin.html`)**: Rich administration environment containing an analytics dashboard (Impressions, CTR, Geolocation demographics, client platform breakdowns), Live Event activity logs, CSV staff directory sync, HTML template importer, and visual campaign banner creator.
* **SaaS Command Center (`super-admin.html`)**: WOLFF operational dashboard with live system logs, database backups, client subscription management, Paystack invoice simulation, and the **SaaS Command Shell Terminal** (runs diagnostic commands and canary deployments).
* **Tracking Telemetry Engine (`/track` API)**: Lightweight endpoints (`open.php`, `click.php`, `logo.php`, `banner.php`) that record telemetry to SQLite without cookies, securing recipient privacy (GDPR and POPIA compliant).

### 6. Revenue Model
MailFooter uses a tiered annual/monthly SaaS subscription model paid in ZAR (R) or USD ($):
* **Starter Plan**: Ideal for small teams (up to 5 users), basic click tracking, and 1 template layout.
* **Team Plan**: Mid-sized teams (up to 20 users), 3 active campaign banners, and 3 layout styles.
* **Enterprise Plan**: Large networks (up to 50 users), unlimited campaign banners, full templates access, and REST API directory syncing.
* **Custom Enterprise**: Teams over 50 users, dedicated support, custom HTML blueprints, and high-performance server allocation.

### 8. Future Strategic Vision
WOLFF's ultimate vision for MailFooter is to solve corporate email headaches by transforming routine outbound communications into a highly effective marketing and awareness tool. 

Future development focuses on:
* **Active Directory / Azure AD Integration**: Automating staff onboarding by syncing directly with corporate directories, eliminating manual profile entry.
* **Automated Invoicing & Paystack webhooks**: Converting simulated payment triggers into real-time accounting updates.
* **AI-Powered Banner Generation**: Integrating design engines (e.g. OpenAI DALL-E) to auto-generate banners based on campaign prompts directly within the Admin Portal.
